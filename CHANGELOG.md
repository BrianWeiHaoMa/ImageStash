# Changelog

Every release of ImageStash and what it changed, newest first, in the [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) shape.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- The pin stays in reach while a picture's preview or the editor is open: it sits at the right end of their top bar, as it does beside the search box, reading "Keep open" or "Unpin". Pinning or unpinning from there keeps the preview on its picture with a name you were still typing, and keeps the edit open with its Undo history and the text you were typing into a layer.
- Drag any edge or corner of the stash window's flyout to resize it, down to the size at which the editor still fits. Every later flyout opens at that size, from the shortcut, the tray or a notification and after Unpin, kept apart from the pinned window's own size and place. Where the display, or the room beside the caret, is too small for it, the flyout opens smaller that time only.
- Trimming a clip shows what you keep: while you drag a Trim handle, or an end of a layer's time bar, the clip pauses on the frame that end is on and stays there when you let go. Play then loops only the kept frames, and the frames cut away can still be shown by clicking the filmstrip or with Left and Right. With Trim chosen, `[` keeps the clip from the frame showing and `]` up to it, each one Undo step.
- Crop by dragging across the part to keep: with Crop chosen, a drag on a picture with no crop, or outside the crop's frame, draws a new frame over what it covers. Each side of the frame has a handle in its middle that moves that side alone, so a crop can be narrowed or widened from one edge. A drag inside the frame still moves it, its corners still resize it, and each drag is one Undo step.
- A drawn arrow or box can be adjusted instead of drawn again: select it with Draw, then drag either end of the arrow to re-aim it, or a corner of the box to resize it, the other end or the opposite corner staying put. Dragging the mark elsewhere still moves it, and each adjustment is one Undo step.
- Undo and Redo are available beside Compare in the editor. The trash button beside the tool options deletes the selected layer.
- Right-click a live tag chip or an entry in Every tag and choose Rename tag… to correct its spelling or merge it into another tag. Type one word and press Enter, or press Esc to cancel.
- Every tag shows how many pictures in your stash carry each tag. The counts appear in the chip row's list, the selection's tag field and the preview's tag field, even while a search is active.
- Right-click a live tag chip or an entry in Every tag to remove that tag from every picture carrying it. The menu gives the stash-wide count, and the notice offers Undo even when only one picture changed.

- Apply a tag or favorite change to a selection of two or more pictures, or drop tiles onto a tag chip or Favorites. A notice shows how many pictures changed. Its Undo takes back the tag changes or restores the favorite flags from that batch. In the grid or in the preview outside its fields, `Ctrl+Z` runs the newest available Undo.
- Search matches names, tags and words saved in Meme, Label and Caption bar layers, including captions from earlier versions. Press Enter on a caption match to hand off the finished picture.
- Hold Shift with an arrow to extend a range of pictures in the grid. Reverse direction to shrink the range, or keep going past its starting picture. Shift+Up and Shift+Down also work from the search box, keeping its text selection.
- Tab to the grid, then use Home or End to select the first or last picture, or Page Up and Page Down to move by one visible page of rows. The picture you reach scrolls into view and is ready to copy, paste or preview.
- From the search box or grid, `Ctrl+1` opens Recent, `Ctrl+2` opens Favorites and `Ctrl+3` opens All. Each clears the search and selects the first picture in that scope. `Ctrl+,` opens Settings. Hover a scope chip or the gear to see its key.
- Left and Right move through the pictures while the search box is empty. With the first picture selected, Right, Right, Enter hands over the third without leaving the box. Actions pressed after an arrow use the selection it reaches, even when the list is still loading.
- When you pause shortcuts, the stash footer and Settings say so and offer Resume. The tray tooltip says shortcuts are paused and still lists unavailable shortcuts, shortening conflict details to fit. The tray icon shows a pause badge or a warning badge for an unavailable shortcut, with the red dot taking precedence while recording.

- A copy now says it worked: after `Ctrl+C`, an Enter that copies (with no app to paste into), or the item menu's Copy, the footer reads "✓ Copied" with the picture's name, or how many pictures, for two seconds before its hints return. In the preview, the button that copies reads "Copied ✓" for a moment instead. A paste into a chat shows nothing of the kind: ImageStash cannot see whether it arrived.
- `Ctrl+Z` now undoes from the keyboard: in the grid, or in a picture's preview outside its name and tag fields, it runs the Undo of the newest notice that still offers one, so a picture deleted with Del comes back with one key, and pressed again it undoes the change before that. Held down, it undoes once. In the search box it still undoes your typing, and in the editor your last edit; notifications above the tray are never undone by it. A notice's Undo button names the key in its tooltip.
- Tagging has a key: `Ctrl+T`, from the search box or the grid, opens "Tags of …" on the selected pictures, so a word typed there and Enter tags every one of them; Esc puts you back where you were, your search as you left it. In a picture's preview, `Ctrl+T` goes straight to the preview's tag box, whose empty box now reads "Add a tag · Ctrl+T". A picture's right-click menu has "Add tags…" too, right after Rename (with several selected, beside Add tag ▸), opening the same "Tags of …"; Add tag ▸ is still there for one-click picks.
- In either tag field, press Backspace in the empty box to highlight the last tag, then press it again to remove that tag. Tab or Shift+Tab reaches a tag's ×. Down in the empty box opens Every tag, where the arrows move between tags and Enter toggles the focused tag.
- Favorites now show: a favorite's tile carries a small white star in its bottom-right corner, and a picture's preview has a star button before ⋯, filled in the accent color while the picture is a favorite and outlined while it is not. A click on it adds or removes the picture, as `Ctrl+D` there does; its tooltip says which, and a screen reader hears whether it is in Favorites. So `Ctrl+D` in the grid, a drop on the Favorites chip and the right-click menu's Add to or Remove from Favorites each show what they did. A star changes once the change is saved, and stays as it was when saving fails, which a notice names. The menu's Remove from Favorites now shows the filled star.
- The arrows now step through pictures in the preview: Left or Up shows the previous picture of the grid, Right or Down the next, in the order the grid lists them (your search's results, Recent, Favorites or All), without closing the preview, and held down they keep stepping. The picture stepped to is the one selected, so Enter, `Ctrl+C`, Del, `Ctrl+D`, F2 and `Ctrl+E` act on it and Esc leaves it selected in the grid. At the first or last picture the step that way does nothing. Inside the preview's name or tag box the arrows stay the box's own (its caret, its suggestions); tab out first, which saves what you typed.
- The preview has a ‹ and a › button over the left and right edges of its picture, which step to the previous and the next picture as Left and Right do, for the mouse. A name or tag you were typing in the preview is saved first, to the picture you typed it for, as Enter would (the highlighted suggestion when one is highlighted); if it cannot be saved, a notice says so and the preview stays on that picture. Both stay faint until the mouse is over the picture or the space around it; each is fainter still and does nothing at the first or the last picture. They take no room from the picture, which still shows at its own size, and a click on one does not pause an animation.
- Copied words can be pasted into a caption: while you type in a text or caption-bar layer in the editor, `Ctrl+V` puts the clipboard's words in where the caret is, in place of any selected text, as if typed: in capitals on a Meme, each copied line a line of the caption. From there it never adds anything to your stash: when the clipboard holds a picture or files instead of words, a notice says it holds no text to paste. `Ctrl+Z` takes pasted words back with the rest of what you typed.
- Paste a copied hex color into the editor's color picker with `Ctrl+V`, then press Enter to apply it. With focus in the hex box, pasting files or a picture shows a notice and leaves the stash alone. If the hex box or caption field closes before the clipboard text arrives, a notice says why the paste was dropped.
- The empty stash, which a new install opens on, now says how to get ImageStash back: a line under its list says it opens from any app with the open shortcut (`Ctrl+Alt+Space` unless you changed it) or from its icon in the tray, and that closing the window keeps it running. Its ways in are now rows, each led by its icon and ending in its shortcut in key caps, and a click on a row does it, as the shortcut, the `+` menu or the tray menu does: Paste a copied image, Take a screenshot, Record a clip, Stash the clipboard image, Import files. A shortcut changed in Settings shows there at once.
- Starting ImageStash yourself now opens its window: from the Start Menu or its `ImageStash.Windows.exe`, after a Quit or an upgrade, the window shows above the tray as a click on the tray icon shows it, instead of nothing happening but a hidden tray icon, and it shows even when the stash could not be opened, saying why. The start Windows makes when you sign in still shows nothing, and no longer brings forward an ImageStash you already started: its Start with Windows entry now carries `--at-sign-in` after the program's path, which each start, the Settings switch and an import write. The first sign-in after replacing an older copy's folder, before you start the new one, opens the window that once. A brand-new install still opens pinned on its empty stash, however it was started.
- The installer now starts ImageStash once it has installed, upgraded or repaired it, so after a first install you see the window it opens on, and after installing a new version over a running one its tray icon and shortcuts are back at once, the window showing above the tray, instead of staying gone until you start it or sign in again. Silent installs (`msiexec /qn`) start it too. Uninstalling starts nothing.
- Pointing at ImageStash's icon in the tray now names the shortcut that opens it, "ImageStash · Ctrl+Alt+Space" (or the combination you set in Settings, as soon as you set it), so the icon reminds you of the one key to remember. It names it only while that key works: not while Pause shortcuts is checked, and not while the shortcut is unavailable, when the icon says which shortcut and why instead, as before.
- Pictures a browser hands over under an unusual name now come in. A JPEG named `.jfif`, `.jpe` or `.pjpeg` is added as the JPEG it is and kept as a `.jpg`, so a chat you paste it into gets a `.jpg`. A file whose name has no extension is looked inside: when it holds a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP it is added, named by its whole file name and kept under `.png`, `.jpg`, `.gif` or `.webp`; anything else is skipped as a file without an extension, as before. Before you let go, the drop card counts files with no extension apart, as "to check" ("1 supported file · 2 to check"), since what they hold is known only once you drop them. This holds for a drop, a folder, pasted files and Import files…, whose Pictures filter lists the JPEG names too; every other file is still judged by its name alone, so a `.mp4` or `.txt` is refused whatever it holds.
- A picture can be dragged out of a browser or a chat straight onto the flyout, without pinning the window first, so an animated GIF comes in whole from wherever you found it. Pressing on the picture used to hide the flyout at once; now the flyout stays while the mouse button is held, shows the drop card when the picture is over it, takes the drop, and then hides, the import's notice waiting for the next time you open it. A click in another app therefore hides the flyout as the button comes up rather than as it goes down; switching apps with the keyboard hides it at once, as before, and the pinned window is unchanged.
- Settings now says which version is running, in a faint line at the foot of the page ("ImageStash 0.2.2"), which you can select and copy for a bug report or to compare with the releases page. It is only text: ImageStash still checks for nothing.
- Import stash… can save your current stash first: once you have chosen the export to import, Export current stash first… beside Replace and Cancel opens the save picker, writes your stash as Export stash… does, and brings you back to the same question, nothing replaced until you press Replace. It will not write over the very file waiting to be imported.
- Each shortcut in Settings that is no longer its default has a small Reset before its field, which puts the default back (`Ctrl+Alt+Space`, `Ctrl+PrtScn`, `Ctrl+Shift+PrtScn` or `Win+Alt+V`), working at once and saved, and then goes. When the default can't be had, because another app uses it, your keyboard layout types a character with it, or another of ImageStash's shortcuts already uses it, the row says why in the same words a pressed combination gets, and you keep the shortcut you had. Tab reaches it like any button, and a field that was listening stops first.
- A shortcut ImageStash cannot have now says so where you look, as soon as it finds out: when another app already holds it, or it would type a character on your keyboard layout, at start or later, a notification above the tray reads "Ctrl+PrtScn is used by another app, so Take a screenshot has no shortcut." with Choose another, which opens Settings on that shortcut (or brings Settings forward there). It never takes the focus from the app you are in, and says it once, not again each time ImageStash checks, unless the shortcut changes, the reason changes, or it worked in between. Until it works again, the tray menu and the window's `+` menu still show the shortcut beside its entry and add "(shortcut unavailable)", the empty stash adds "unavailable" after its key caps, and its line about opening ImageStash offers only the tray icon; each entry and row still does its act.

### Changed

- When ImageStash won't do what you asked, or can't do it yet, it says so in one plain sentence instead of reporting that something failed: "Can't paste: the clipboard holds no picture or file." or "Can't take a screenshot (the screen): your stash is still opening." This covers pasting or stashing a clipboard with no picture, a dropped link, an unreadable picture, a picture no longer in your stash, a folder Move… can't use, a file that isn't an export, an Undo that no longer applies, and anything asked while the stash opens or is being moved, exported or imported.
- When something goes wrong, ImageStash says what it couldn't do, to what, and why, in one sentence that ends once, instead of "Rename failed: cat.png, another program is holding it open": "Couldn't rename: cat.png is held open by another program. Try again in a moment." Only a file another program holds adds that last sentence, after ImageStash has already tried again for a few seconds. Where Windows gives its own words for a problem, a plain explanation comes first ("Couldn't import files: the file picker did not open (…)"). The tray icon's tooltip speaks the same way, shortening a long failure to "Couldn't open the stash"; when several failures cut that short still do not fit, they share one line ("Couldn't open the stash, read settings or save settings") so each is still named. Open with… problems read "Couldn't open with another app".
- The first time you pin the flyout, the pinned window opens where the flyout was, on the same display and wholly on screen, instead of in the middle of the main display. It comes back to that place each time you pin it until you move it yourself. A brand-new install still opens its empty stash pinned in the middle of the main display.
- The flyout now has an edge all the way round, a thin grey line with rounded corners like Windows 11's own windows, so it no longer blends into a white chat behind it, in the light and the dark look. The pinned window keeps the system's frame.
- Leaving the editor without changing anything no longer puts up a "Discarded the edit" notice, whether you press Cancel, the back arrow, Discard changes or Esc. After a change, Cancel and the back arrow still leave at once with the notice and its Undo, while Esc first says "Press Esc again to discard your changes" in the hint line for two seconds, and only a second Esc in that time leaves. Inside the editor, holding Esc down counts as one press.
- A blur now grows stronger with the size of its box, so it softens words on a small screenshot as much as on a large one, and no blur is weaker than before. A blur saved by an earlier version takes the new strength the next time its picture is saved. Hide's hint says Black box is the style that leaves nothing in the finished picture.
- A new Meme, Label or Caption bar comes in the font, fill and outline you last chose in this edit for one of its kind, so the bottom line of a meme matches the top without choosing them again. Each kind keeps its own: a Caption bar still starts with black letters on its white bar after a white Meme. Each edit starts again from Impact for a Meme and Segoe UI for the others.
- A long Meme line now shrinks as you type so that it stays on two lines, down to half the size it started at, and grows back as you delete letters. A top Meme keeps its top edge and a bottom one its bottom edge. Once you resize a Meme by a corner, it keeps that size and wraps as before. Undo takes back the text and the size together.
- Recent keeps pictures in place when you paste from the pinned window, copy or drag them out. New arrivals join the end. Opening ImageStash again or choosing a scope applies the latest order. With an empty search, tag chips likewise hold their places. New tags join the end and a tag that loses its last picture leaves a dim, inactive chip until the next reorder. Every tag lists the current tags by how many pictures carry them.
- Tag chips stay in place when clicked, so a second click at the same spot removes the filter. Newly made tags join the end of that row once the last filter is removed. Clicks can complete the unfinished last word, so `cat jo` becomes `cat joy` and choosing vacation after cat keeps both. After a click, tags absent from the results dim and remain available in Every tag. You can still drop tiles onto a dim chip while its tag exists.
- Hovering a tile gives it a subtle outline. Rest the pointer there to see the picture's name, up to three tags and a star if it's a favorite. Your selection stays where it was.

- A shortcut Settings refuses now says what is wrong and what to do, in a sentence: "Ctrl+PrtScn is already used by another app. Press a different combination.", "Ctrl+Alt+Q types @ on your keyboard layout, so it can't be a shortcut." (naming the character your layout types, when it shows as one), "Ctrl+V is the paste key ImageStash presses, so it can't be a shortcut.", "Take a screenshot already uses Ctrl+Alt+S." The field keeps listening after a refusal, still reading "Press a new shortcut…", so you just press another combination: no click on the field again. Esc, its ×, Tab or clicking elsewhere stops it as before, and the refusal goes with it; while it listens, pressing another of ImageStash's shortcuts is refused there rather than doing what it does. A key held down no longer counts as a second try.
- A shortcut ImageStash cannot have reads the same way under its Settings row, and the tray icon's tooltip names it in one sentence, the combination, why, and which act is left without a shortcut: "Ctrl+PrtScn is used by another app, so Take a screenshot has no shortcut." When several are named and the tooltip cannot hold every sentence, each still names its shortcut, and those that fit keep their why, the newest first.
- The tray icon's tooltip names each shortcut ImageStash cannot have once, however often it is checked again; importing settings that bring back a combination another app holds no longer lists it twice.
- A screenshot, a recording or a picture stashed with Stash clipboard image now shows in Recent straight away, placed by when you made it, so with the stash window on Recent it is the first picture you see at the next shortcut, instead of one to find in All. Once you paste, copy or drag it out, it is placed by that use like every other picture. Pictures you drop, import or paste into the window still join Recent only once used, and captures made by an earlier version are not marked as captures. Making a capture never moves what you had selected. A stash this version has opened, and an export it writes, can no longer be opened by an earlier version.
- Stash clipboard image's shortcut now defaults to `Win+Alt+V` instead of `Ctrl+Alt+V`, which is Paste Special in Excel and PowerPoint and a paste key in Word, so that key does what those apps mean by it again while ImageStash runs. If you already use ImageStash you keep the shortcut your settings hold, `Ctrl+Alt+V` unless you changed it, until you record another in Settings; an export carries yours along. A shortcut with the Windows key is now written with Win first in Settings, on the empty stash and in the tray icon's tooltip, as Windows writes its own, and Settings shows each shortcut in that same spelling.
- A shortcut field no longer takes a key every app needs: Delete, Insert, Home, End, Page Up or Down, an arrow, Space, a letter or F1 to F12, pressed without Ctrl, Alt or the Windows key (with Shift alone too), is refused on the spot with "Add Ctrl, Alt or Win: Delete alone is needed by every app.", and the field keeps listening for another. PrtScn, Pause, Scroll Lock and F13 to F24 can still be shortcuts on their own. Such a key that your settings already hold, from an earlier version or an imported export, keeps working until you change it.

- The Settings page reads from the everyday to the risky: Shortcuts; Screenshots and recordings (Also copy it to the clipboard, Maximum length, and Record the mouse pointer, which was Show the mouse pointer); Appearance and startup (Theme, Thumbnail size, and Start with Windows, now described as "Start ImageStash when you sign in, so its shortcuts always work"); Your stash, with where it is stored, Open folder and Move…; and last, Move to another PC, with Export stash… and Import stash…. Nothing is called "Library" or "General" any more, and Theme and Thumbnail size no longer sit at the bottom.
- Import stash…'s question now says what you lose: "Replace your 1,240 items with the 85 in ImageStash export 2026-11-02.zip?", counting your whole stash whatever the grid is showing, then that your current pictures, tags and edits leave ImageStash, which settings the file's replace, and that replaced files go to the Recycle Bin, which may not keep a large stash; the Import row's line says the same of the Recycle Bin. Its Import button is now Replace, a plain button like Cancel rather than the highlighted one. If the stash changes before you press Replace, the question is asked again with the new count.

- The chip row under the search box makes room for tags: Recent, Favorites and All are now small chips showing a clock, a star and a grid (hover one, or ask a screen reader, for its name), and tag chips are a little tighter, so the stash window at its default size shows about twice as many tags before the rest move to the last chip. That last chip, which lists every tag, now shows a tag and a chevron instead of "…". The row is the same in the flyout and the pinned window.
- A long import no longer pulls the window out from under you. If, while pictures are still coming in, you type in the search box, click a scope or a tag chip, move the selection, or open a picture's preview, the import's end leaves all of that as you left it: the grid still shows the new pictures wherever they belong in what you are looking at, and the import's notice offers Show beside its Undo, which clears the search and shows All with the new pictures selected, closing the preview (an open edit stays open). If you touched nothing, the import ends as before, the search cleared and All showing the new pictures first and selected. A drop of pictures that were all already in your stash now selects them in All and scrolls to them, instead of leaving the selection where it was.
- Adding pictures while a tag chip is lit now tags every one of them and says so. A drop, Import files… (`Ctrl+O`) or a paste gives the lit tags to each new picture and now also to a picture your stash already held, which is still not added a second time. The notice names the tags: "Added 4 to your stash, tagged #cat", and "cat.png was already in your stash as “cat”. This import gave it #funny." for a picture that did not carry the tag yet. Its Undo takes back everything the import did, the pictures it added and each tag it gave a picture already there, and is offered even when every picture was already in your stash. Before you let go, the drop card says each picture gets the tag, those already in your stash too, and the file picker's title names it, "Import files (tagged #cat)". A picture already in your stash that has no name is named by its file in that notice, instead of by empty quotes.
- With several pictures selected, the footer now says how many Enter hands over ("Enter · Paste 3 items into Slack", or "Enter · Copy 3 items"), and so do the item menu's paste and Copy entries, so a selection left over from tidying is never pasted unannounced.

- Search now lists first the pictures where every word you typed starts a word of the name or of a tag, then the rest, and within each, the ones you used most recently first, then those never used, newest first. So typing `re` and Enter pastes the "really" reaction you post every day, not the newest screenshot, whose name holds those letters only inside a word. Everything search found before it still finds. Favorites and All still list newest-added first.
- After a paste hides the stash window, the next shortcut opens it on Recent with an empty search box, the picture you just pasted selected on its own (first, unless you have made a capture since), so "shortcut, Enter" pastes it again and two typed letters start a fresh search. Hiding it any other way (clicking back into the chat, the shortcut, Esc) still leaves your search and selection as they were. The pinned window is left as it is.
- A preview open in the stash window now closes when the window hides, so the next shortcut lands in the search box and typed letters search, instead of finding the old preview, where Enter would paste that picture again. The pinned window keeps its preview; so does a preview whose name or tag you were still typing, and one a screenshot or recording hid for a moment and put back.
- After importing several pictures, they are no longer all still selected the next time you open the window, where Enter would paste them all: the first of them alone is. Several pictures you selected yourself stay selected.
- `Ctrl+C` right after the shortcut opens the window now copies the selected picture, as the footer says, instead of the leftover search word the window had selected so that typing replaces it. Text you select in the search box yourself (by dragging, Shift with an arrow, Home or End, or `Ctrl+A`) is still copied as text.
- A notice in the stash window now stays as long as its words take to read: six seconds for a short one, and for a longer one two seconds plus one for every three and a half words, up to twenty. Its time runs only while the window is showing, so a result that arrives while the window is hidden or minimised, such as an import that ends after you switched back to your chat, is still there, with its whole time ahead of it, when you next open the window. Move…, Export stash… and Import stash… don't wait on a notice in a hidden or minimised window: its Undo ends as if its time were up, and they go ahead once what that ends has finished. Notifications above the tray still stay six seconds.
- A notice in the stash window that names a problem (a file skipped or unreadable, a refusal, a failure) now stays until you close it with its ×, or until you next do the same thing (another paste, another drop, another delete), whose notice takes its place, so a skipped file or a failed delete is still on screen when you look back. Its Undo still lasts only for the notice's time: once that is up, the Undo button goes and the words stay. A notice that names no problem, such as "Deleted …", leaves at its time as before, and notifications above the tray still leave after six seconds.
- A tag or a name you typed is no longer thrown away when you move on: in a preview's tag box, in "Tags of …", or in a preview's name while you edit it, clicking the grid, the search box, or the preview's picture, name or buttons, pressing Tab, or an import's end or its notice's Show closing the preview, adds the tag, or renames the picture, as Enter would (the highlighted suggestion when one is highlighted), and the click still does what you clicked, with focus where you put it. Esc still cancels. Switching to another app keeps what you typed in a preview for when you come back, as before.
- An import's notice now leads with a semi-bold line of counts ("Added 38 · 1 already in your stash · 1 skipped", or "Added 3 to your stash" when nothing else happened), then names only the files that were not added, each with its reason, so a skipped or failed file is the first thing you read instead of sitting below forty names. The added pictures are named only when there are three or fewer; they are already selected in the grid.
- A screenshot or clip edited from its notification can now go straight into the chat you took it over: Edit, Trim or a click on the notification opens the window with the app that was in front just before your click as where Enter pastes, so once you caption the picture and Save, the footer reads "Enter · Paste into Discord" (or whichever app it was) and Enter pastes the finished picture there. Nothing is pasted until you press Enter. When ImageStash's own window, the desktop or the taskbar was in front before the click, Enter copies, as before; opening the window from the tray still copies too.
- A capture's notification now reads at a glance. Its title says what you captured, "Screenshot stashed", "Recording stashed" or "Clipboard image stashed", and one line under it gives the facts: a recording's length first, then, when ImageStash put it on the clipboard, "On the clipboard · Ctrl+V pastes it", then the file's size ("2.4 s · On the clipboard · Ctrl+V pastes it · 1.1 MB"). The name is drawn as the field it is, boxed with its rename icon, so the "Click the name to change it" hint is gone and Edit (or Trim) and Undo have their row to themselves; a long name ends in an ellipsis. A stashed clipboard image's notification still says that a pasted picture is always a still. The notification's title, which screen readers read, gives the title, the name as captured and the line without the size: "Screenshot stashed: “Screenshot 2026-11-24 10.15.03”. On the clipboard · Ctrl+V pastes it."
- The recording bar no longer moves when you click Record: it has one layout from the moment the region waits, so Record turns into Stop in the same spot and the pointer that clicked Record is already on Stop. Cancel now reads "Cancel" instead of a bare ×, and sits at the bar's far left, away from Stop, so a slip at the end of a good take does not throw it away. The second click of a double-click on Record no longer stops the recording it just started; a click on Stop after that, the record shortcut and the tray's Stop recording end it as before. The bar also names the record shortcut, "Ctrl+Shift+PrtScn starts and stops" while the region waits and "… stops" while it records, as you have it set in Settings, following a pause or a change made while it shows; it names none while shortcuts are paused or the record shortcut is unavailable, nor one changed while it shows to a longer one than its room holds.
- A screenshot or a clip is on the clipboard sooner while Also copy a capture to the clipboard is on: as soon as it is saved in your stash, instead of after the stash window has been listed again and brought back, so a `Ctrl+V` right after the capture finds it. A clip also becomes a picture of your stash sooner after Stop: its size, frames and length are taken from what was recorded as it was written, instead of opening the saved GIF again and reading every frame. ## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-10

### Added

- A stash of your reaction pictures and GIFs that lives in the Windows 11 tray and starts with Windows.
- The paste moment: `Ctrl+Alt+Space` opens the stash beside the text caret, two typed letters find a picture, and Enter pastes it into the chat you were typing in without ever sending the message.
- Filling the stash: drop files, a folder or pictures from a browser onto the window, paste a copied picture with `Ctrl+V`, import files with `Ctrl+O`, screenshot a region with `Ctrl+PrtScn`, record a few seconds of the screen as a GIF with `Ctrl+Shift+PrtScn`, and stash the clipboard's picture with `Ctrl+Alt+V`.
- PNG and JPEG as stills (an animated PNG is handled as a still), and GIF and WebP, still or animated, kept as the files they came as.
- Finding pictures again by thumbnail, name, tag, favorite and recent use, with tag chips and a search that narrows as you type.
- Renaming, tagging, favoriting, multi-selecting and deleting with Undo; dragging a picture out as a file; copying with `Ctrl+C`; Save a copy, Open with and Reveal in File Explorer.
- An editor with five tools (Text, Crop, Trim, Hide and Draw) whose edits stay as layers over the untouched original, so every edit can be changed or reverted later.
- Settings with the four shortcuts, Also copy a capture, the recording's length and pointer, the stash's location with Move, Start with Windows and Theme.
- Export stash and Import stash, to carry the whole stash and its settings to another PC or account.
- An MSI installer that installs ImageStash for the signed-in user without administrator rights, upgrades it in place and removes it again, and a zip of the same folder to copy onto a PC and run.
